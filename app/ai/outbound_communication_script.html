The core flow has 4 mandatory phases — Opening → Order Review → Update Check → Final Confirmation. Never skip the Order Review phase even if the customer seems ready to confirm.

Phase 2 — Order review (read all details aloud)
Never skip this
Always read the full order details before asking for confirmation. This is the most important phase — it catches wrong items, wrong addresses, and prevents RTO before dispatch.
read the complete order
Aria
"Great! So let me quickly read out your order details. You've ordered [Product Name], [Quantity] piece — [Color / Size / Variant]. The total amount is ₹[Amount] [payable at delivery / already paid]. And the delivery address we have is [City and State only]. Does everything sound correct so far?"
customer says yes
Customer
"Yes, that's correct."
Aria — move to Phase 3
"Perfect! Now before I confirm your order — would you like to make any changes? Like the quantity, colour, size, or the delivery address?"
customer says something is wrong

Aria

Log the change. Update in Shopify. Then continue to Phase 3.
Read city and state only — never read the full street address on call. If they need to verify the full address, ask them to confirm it. "Could you confirm the full address you'd like it delivered to?"

Key step

Aria

if no changes needed

address confirmed — move to final confirmation

if COD order — clarify payment
Aria (COD only)

The update check is what separates a good outbound call from a great one. It prevents wrong deliveries, size mismatches, and COD returns before they happen.

Always ask WHY before cancelling. One question saves up to 20% of cancellations. Offer a fix. If the customer still wants to cancel — respect it immediately without pushing further.

Silence / no response timing protocol
Follow exactly
0–4 seconds → stay silent, normal connection lag
At 5 seconds
"Hello? Am I speaking with [Name]?"
At 12 seconds
"Hi there — I can hear some background noise. Take your time, I'm right here!"
At 20 seconds
"I'm not able to hear you clearly — there might be a connection issue. You can call us back at [number] or we'll reach out on WhatsApp. Have a good day!"
30 seconds → disconnect. Log as No Answer. Send WhatsApp. Tag: Retry Call

Scan transcript for confirm/cancel keywords. If clear confirm found → tag Proceed + note "Intent clear from transcript, call dropped." If unclear → Retry Call → send WhatsApp.
hung up after saying cancel
Do NOT auto-cancel. Send WhatsApp: "Hi [Name], we got disconnected. Did you mean to cancel your order for [Product]? Reply CANCEL to confirm cancellation, or KEEP to keep it."
Cancellation always needs explicit re-confirmation even if transcript shows cancel keyword. Confirmation can be inferred — cancellation cannot. Asymmetric for safety.
